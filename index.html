CURRICULUM MODULES

Build the Skills Behind a Strong First Impression

Every session topic delivers actionable techniques to turn your academic training into confident professional presentation.

Module 01

Resume & Profile Presentation

Turn duty lists into high-impact accomplishment bullets and structure CV and LinkedIn profiles around measurable outcomes.

Module 02

Interview Preparation

Research companies and interview formats, then ask strategic questions that show genuine interest and preparedness.

Module 03

Professional Communication

Refine your tone, pacing, body language, and virtual presence with concise speaking structures that keep interviewers engaged.

Module 04

Strengths & Transferable Skills

Identify your signature strengths and link coursework, leadership roles, and background to real workplace problems.

Module 05

Answering Behavioural Questions

Use the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to answer tough situational and conflict-resolution questions.

Module 06

Demonstrating Projects & Impact

Walk evaluators through case studies, code repos, and capstone work, explaining your reasoning, trade-offs, and value.
